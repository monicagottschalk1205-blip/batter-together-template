Questions? I've Got Answers

How far ahead should I order?

Two weeks is best for cakes, cookies and cupcakes. Wedding cakes and dessert tables need 1–3 months. Need something sooner? Send a request anyway, and if I have room I'll squeeze you in!

Can you make a cake like a picture I found?

Yes! Send me your inspiration photos and I'll create my own version that fits your colors, size and budget.

Do you deliver?

Free pickup is from my home in Kansas City. Local delivery is available for a small fee based on distance, and wedding cakes and dessert tables include delivery and setup.

What about allergies?

I offer gluten-free, dairy-free and egg-free options for most treats. Just let me know when you order. My kitchen isn't allergen-free, so please tell me about any serious allergies.

What flavors do you offer?

Favorites include vanilla bean, chocolate fudge, lemon raspberry, strawberries & cream, red velvet, funfetti and cookies & cream. Have a special flavor in mind? Ask!

How do I pay, and what if I need to cancel?

A 50% deposit holds your date, and the rest is due at pickup. Cash, Venmo or card are all fine. Cancel at least 7 days ahead and your deposit can roll over to a future order.
